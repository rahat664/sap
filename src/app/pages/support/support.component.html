Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

How to use Ecoflit?

App Version

  1. Open the Google Play Store (for Android devices).
  2. Search for "Ecoflit" in the search bar.
  3. Select the Ecoflit app or click here.
  4. Tap the Install button.
  5. Follow on-screen instructions to complete the installation.

Note: The app will soon be available for iOS devices.

Web Version

For customers: https://ecoflit.com/

How will a new customer/user log in?

  • Mobile number & OTP: Log in using a mobile number and verify via OTP.
  • Email & Password: Use credentials sent to the provided email address.

Can I change my password?

No, currently passwords cannot be changed. This feature will be available soon.

How can a customer/admin give account access to other team members?

Web Version

  1. Navigate to User Management: Click "User Management" from the left-side taskbar.
  2. Click "Add New User" and fill in:
    • Full Name
    • Mobile Number
    • Email
    • Designation
    • User Type (Admin/User)
  3. Click "Save" to create the account. Credentials will be sent via email.
